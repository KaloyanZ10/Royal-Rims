About Royal Rims

Style That Defines
Every Drive.

Royal Rims brings together premium alloy wheels, custom finishes, and professional restoration for drivers who see every detail as part of the car's identity.

From bold new designs to refined finishes and careful wheel repairs, our work is shaped by clean presentation, individual style, and a precise eye for how the final result sits on the road.

Premium Design
Carefully selected wheel styles that elevate the presence and character of each vehicle.
Custom Finishes
Finishes tailored to the look of the car, from subtle elegance to stand-out personal statements.
Restoration Expertise
Professional restoration that brings worn, marked, or damaged wheels back to a clean, confident finish.
Detail & Precision
Every choice is handled with attention to fitment, finish, balance, and the way the wheel completes the build.
